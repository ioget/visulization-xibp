# Coach's Insight Hub

XamCoach — Frontend, UI, UX & Design System Master Prompt

1. Product Vision

Design and implement XamCoach, a premium AI-powered basketball coaching intelligence platform for professional and semi-professional women's basketball teams in the French league ecosystem.

This is not an admin dashboard and should not look like a CRUD application.

The product should feel like a professional sports-intelligence workspace used by a coaching staff before training, before a game, and during post-game analysis.

Think:

Hudl × Sportlogiq × Linear × modern financial/BI analytics

The interface must communicate:

precision

speed

trust

basketball intelligence

professional performance analysis

clarity under time pressure

The coach should be able to understand the important information within 5–10 seconds of opening a screen.

2. Core UX Principle

DATA ≠ AI

This distinction must be visible throughout the entire product.

Verified / computed data

Examples:

points

rebounds

assists

FG%

3P%

usage

shot locations

game scores

player rankings

league comparisons

charts

tables

These come from the database and are ground truth.

Visual treatment:

crisp

dense

structured

strong typography

tabular numerals

blue data accent

minimal decorative elements

Never make numerical data look conversational or speculative.

AI-generated information

Examples:

tactical briefing

SWOT analysis

development plan

game interpretation

coaching recommendations

narrative summaries

These are generated by AI and must always be visually distinguishable.

Use a subtle but consistent visual language:

small AI GENERATED badge

small sparkle/intelligence icon

slightly different card background

optional blue/purple intelligence indicator

Sources / Evidence affordance

Do NOT make AI content visually louder than the actual data.

The hierarchy should always be:

DATA → INTERPRETATION → ACTION

3. Design Personality

The visual language should be:

Professional

Looks suitable for a professional coaching staff.

Athletic

Subtle basketball/sports references without becoming a basketball-themed gaming website.

Intelligent

Feels like an analytical product rather than a generic dashboard.

Calm

Avoid excessive gradients, glowing effects, neon colors, glassmorphism, or excessive animation.

Dense but readable

The product contains a lot of information, but information density must be controlled through hierarchy rather than simply shrinking everything.

Premium

Every spacing, border, typography, icon and interaction should feel intentional.

Avoid:

generic SaaS templates

excessive rounded cards

giant empty dashboards

excessive shadows

cartoon basketball graphics

unnecessary gradients

excessive orange

huge typography

decorative elements that don't improve usability

38. UX Quality Bar

Before considering a screen finished, ask:

Can a coach understand the screen within 5 seconds?

Is the most important information visually dominant?

Can I distinguish database data from AI interpretation immediately?

Can I understand where AI claims came from?

Can I complete the main action without searching around?

Is the screen dense without feeling cluttered?

Does it look like professional sports software?

Does the design remain consistent with other screens?

Are loading, empty and error states handled?

Does the interface feel fast even when AI operations take time?

38. UX Quality Bar

Before considering a screen finished, ask:

Can a coach understand the screen within 5 seconds?

Is the most important information visually dominant?

Can I distinguish database data from AI interpretation immediately?

Can I understand where AI claims came from?

Can I complete the main action without searching around?

Is the screen dense without feeling cluttered?

Does it look like professional sports software?

Does the design remain consistent with other screens?

Are loading, empty and error states handled?

Does the interface feel fast even when AI operations take time?

39. Overall Visual Target

The final result should feel like:

A professional coaching intelligence platform — not an AI chatbot, not a generic dashboard, and not a spreadsheet.

The visual hierarchy should communicate:

                 XamCoach
                    ↓
             Basketball Data
                    ↓
            AI Interpretation
                    ↓
             Coaching Action


The product should make coaches feel that the system has transformed complex basketball data into something they can immediately understand and act upon.

Prioritize clarity, trust, speed, data precision, and professional sports aesthetics over visual decoration.
